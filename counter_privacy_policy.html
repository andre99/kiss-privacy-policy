Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Counter respects your privacy. This application does not collect, store, transmit, or request any personally identifiable information from its users.

1. Information Storage

All counters created within the application are stored locally on your device. No counter names, values, actions, or other user-generated content are transmitted to or stored on servers operated by the developer.

The developer does not have access to any data created within the application.

2. Third-Party Services

Firebase

Counter uses Firebase services to collect anonymous crash reports and usage statistics that help improve application stability and performance.

No counter names, counter values, or user-generated content are included in analytics or crash reports.

Google AdMob

The free version of Counter displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

AdMob may use the device advertising identifier and other information to provide personalized or non-personalized advertisements, subject to your device settings and applicable laws.

No counter information or user-generated content is shared with AdMob.

You can manage your advertising preferences through your Google account:

https://adssettings.google.com

3. Data Sharing

The application does not sell, rent, or share user-generated data with third parties.

Anonymous technical information may be processed by Firebase and Google AdMob according to their respective privacy policies.

4. Children's Privacy

This application is not specifically directed to children under the age of 13. No personal information is knowingly collected from children.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page together with the updated revision date.
